# Regional Sales Analysis Dashboard (2018–2021)
# 1. Project Overview
The Regional Sales Analysis Dashboard (2018–2021) is an interactive sales analytics project designed to analyze and visualize business performance across four geographical regions: East, West, North, and South.
The dashboard provides insights into regional sales trends, category-wise sales and profit, and customer segment performance. It presents business data through interactive line charts, pie charts, and doughnut charts, allowing users to explore sales patterns and compare performance across regions.
Each regional section includes a manager's name, monthly sales trends, category-wise sales and profit distribution, and segment-wise sales analysis. The dashboard uses a distinct color theme for each region to make the information easier to understand and compare.
# 2. Project Objectives
The main objective of this project is to transform regional sales data into meaningful business insights using data visualization and analytical techniques.
The key objectives are:
- Analyze sales performance across four geographical regions.
- Track monthly sales trends from 2018 to 2021.
- Compare sales and profit across different product categories.
- Understand sales distribution across customer segments.
- Identify changes in sales performance over time.
- Present regional business performance through an interactive dashboard.
- Support data-driven business decisions using visual reports.

# 5. Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
The monthly sales trend section uses line charts to display sales performance across the twelve months of the year.
Separate charts are provided for the East, West, North, and South regions. Each chart presents monthly sales values, helping users identify fluctuations and patterns throughout the year.
Key Features
- Monthly sales visualization from January to December.
- Comparison of sales values across different months.
- Identification of monthly sales increases and decreases.
- Recognition of recurring sales patterns.
- Regional monitoring of monthly sales performance.
Business Insights
Monthly sales analysis can help businesses identify periods of higher and lower sales activity. These insights can support sales planning, inventory management, resource allocation, and promotional strategies.
Comparing the four regions also helps identify differences in their monthly sales patterns.
# 6. Category-Wise Sales and Profit Analysis
The category-wise section uses pie charts to visualize the distribution of sales and profit across product categories.
The dashboard displays the following categories:
- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology
Each regional chart presents category-level values and their percentage contributions, making it easier to compare the relative distribution of business performance.
Key Features
- Category-wise sales and profit visualization.
- Comparison of Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- Display of category values and percentage contributions.
- Identification of categories with larger or smaller contributions.
- Regional comparison of product category performance.
Business Insights
Category-wise analysis helps businesses understand how different product categories contribute to overall performance.
For example, the analysis can help identify categories that account for a larger share of sales and highlight areas that may require further investigation into profitability.
Important: The chart title alone does not establish whether every displayed value represents sales, profit, or a combination of both. Confirm the underlying measures before making specific claims about category profitability.
# 7. Segment-Wise Sales Analysis
The segment-wise section uses doughnut charts to visualize sales distribution across three customer segments.
The dashboard includes:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office
Each regional chart displays sales values and percentage contributions for the three segments. The total shown in the center of each doughnut chart summarizes the regional sales amount.
Key Features
- Customer segment-wise sales analysis.
- Comparison of Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office sales.
- Visualization of segment contributions as percentages.
- Display of total sales for each region.
- Identification of the segments contributing the largest share of regional sales.
Business Insights
Segment-wise analysis helps businesses understand which customer groups contribute most to sales.
These insights can support customer segmentation, targeted marketing, sales planning, and the development of strategies to improve customer engagement.
